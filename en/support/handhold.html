Help – Handhold

Questions about Handhold? Here are the most common answers. If yours isn't here, write to me.

My purchase isn't showing

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore Purchases”, using the Apple Account you bought with. Purchases approved by a parent (Ask to Buy) unlock automatically once approved.

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Handhold in iPhone Settings → Notifications, and that Daily reminders is on in the app's Settings. No reminder comes on a day you've already tapped Done.

Done from a widget or Control Center

Add the Handhold widget from the Home Screen or Lock Screen, or the Done control in Control Center. A Done tapped there shows up in the app the next time you open it.

I missed a day

Nothing breaks. Your chain counts the days you did, and your rest day never counts against you. After two misses the step gets a little smaller, so starting again is easy.

Progress on a new iPhone

With iCloud on, your progress follows you automatically. Sign in with the same Apple Account.
