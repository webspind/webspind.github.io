Help – Loftpass

Questions about Loftpass? Here are the most common answers. If yours isn't here, write to me.

My purchase isn't showing

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore Purchases”, using the Apple Account you bought with. Purchases approved by a parent (Ask to Buy) unlock automatically once approved.

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Loftpass in iPhone Settings → Notifications, and that the reminder is on in the app.

A question looks wrong

Tap “Report a problem with this question” under the explanation. I get the question number and fix it in the next update.

Progress on a new iPhone

With iCloud on, your progress follows you automatically. Sign in with the same Apple Account.
