Hi — Webspind is just me.

Jagtprøven is my first app. I built it because the syllabus for the Danish hunting licence exam is scattered across booklets, websites and statutory orders — and because bird identification is next to impossible to practise on paper.

I write the questions, draw the interface, fix the bugs and read the support mail. That is why support here is a real page and not a form that goes nowhere — write, and you get me.
