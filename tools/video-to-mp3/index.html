Convert video to MP3

Keep just the sound from a video and save it as an MP3 you can play anywhere.

Processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Choose a video. An audio file such as M4A or WAV works too.
  2. Pick a quality. 192 kbps is right for most things.
  3. Press Make MP3 and download the file.

Good to know

The sound is converted on your own device, so a long video can take a while on a phone. This tool is the quickest of the video tools because it doesn't have to touch the picture.

Use it for recordings and videos that are yours or that you may use. It only reads files on your device: it doesn't fetch anything from YouTube or other sites.

Questions

Is my video uploaded?

No. It's read and converted in your browser, on your device.

Which quality should I choose?

192 kbps sounds good for music and speech. Use 128 kbps for a smaller file, such as a talk or a podcast, and 320 kbps if the original sound is very good.

Can it convert files from YouTube?

No, it works only with files you already have on your device.

Is there a file-size limit?

No fixed limit. The tool reads big files without loading them all at once; a phone will just take longer on them.
