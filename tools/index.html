Your files never leave your device

Handy file tools that never upload your files

Everything happens inside your browser, on your own computer or phone. Nothing is sent to a server, so it's fine to use them with private recordings, family videos and photos.

No upload

Your files are opened and changed on your own device. We never see them.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no watermark. Open a page and use it.

Honest about limits

Your device does the work, so very long videos take time. Each page says what to expect.

Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your files are opened and processed in your browser on your own device. Two tools, transcription and background removal, download an AI model the first time you use them. That download is one way: the model comes to you, and your files stay put.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no trial and no watermark.

Why are some tools slower than websites that upload?

Your own device does the work instead of a rented server. That is the price of privacy, and the reason a long video can take a while.
