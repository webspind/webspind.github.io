Convert video to GIF

Pick the moment you want and turn it into a GIF. Your video stays on your device.

Processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Choose a video.
  2. Set where the GIF should start and how long it should be.
  3. Press Make GIF, then check it and download it.

Good to know

GIF is an old format and files get big fast. Keep clips under about 10 seconds and width at 480 pixels for a file you can share. The tool allows up to 60 seconds.

Your device does the work, so a short clip takes a moment, and a long or wide one takes longer.

Questions

Is my video uploaded?

No. It's turned into a GIF in your browser, on your device.

Why is my GIF so big?

GIF has no modern compression. Lower the width, the frames per second or the length, and it shrinks quickly.

Does the GIF have sound?

No. The GIF format has no sound.

Is there a watermark?

No.
