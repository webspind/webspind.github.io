Remove the background from an image

Cut out the person or thing in a photo and save it with a transparent background.

Your photo stays on your device. Only the AI model is downloaded.

How it works

  1. Choose a photo.
  2. Keep the background transparent, or choose white.
  3. Press Remove background, check the result and download the PNG.

Good to know

The work is done on your device by an AI model called U-2-Net. The first time you use it, your browser downloads the model, not your files, about 175 MB. After that it starts from your browser's cache.

On a normal computer one photo takes around ten seconds once the model is downloaded. Phones may run out of memory, so a computer is the better choice for this one.

It works best with a clear subject against a calmer background: a person, a product, a pet. Fine hair, glass and busy backgrounds may need a touch-up, because the model looks at the picture in a reduced size.

Questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The photo stays in your browser on your device. Only the AI model is downloaded, the first time.

Can I use the result commercially?

Yes. The result is yours, and the model is released under the Apache 2.0 licence, which allows commercial use.

Is there a watermark or a limit?

No watermark, no sign-up and no limit on the number of photos.

Why is the first run slow?

It has to download the model first, about 175 MB. Later runs start much faster.
