Resize images

Change the size of one image or a whole batch, by exact pixels or by percent.

Processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Choose one or many images.
  2. Enter a width or height, or a percentage. Leave one side empty to keep the proportions.
  3. Press Resize images and download them, one at a time or as a zip.

Good to know

Everything is done on your device, one image at a time, so a batch can take a little while but stays in your hands.

Choose Crop to fill if you need an exact shape such as a square, or Stretch only if you don't mind the picture being squeezed.

Questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. They're resized in your browser, on your device.

Can I resize many images at once?

Yes. Add as many as you like and download them as a zip.

What's the best size for the web?

A width of 1600 pixels is a good choice for photos on most websites. For email, 1200 pixels is usually plenty.

Does it make images bigger too?

Yes, up to 400 percent, but enlarged images look softer because detail can't be invented.
