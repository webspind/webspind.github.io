Privacy

The Webspind tools run in your browser. The audio, video and photos you choose are opened and processed on your own device. They are not uploaded, stored or seen by anyone.

What your browser downloads

  • The pages and the code that does the work come from webspind.com, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub receives the usual technical request details (such as your IP address) when your browser asks for a page. We don't get them.
  • Two tools use an AI model: Transcribe and Remove background. Each downloads its model, not your files, the first time you use it, from the public model host Hugging Face. Your browser keeps the model in its cache so the next run doesn't need the download.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • Nothing you process is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The models and the program files are kept in your browser's normal cache so the tools start faster next time. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your own files are only held in the tab's memory while you use the tool, and are gone when you press Start over or close the tab.

The open-source parts we use and their licences are listed in LICENSES.md. Questions? Write to webspind@gmail.com.
