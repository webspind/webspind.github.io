Convert HEIC to JPG

iPhones save photos as HEIC, which many programs and websites won't open. Turn them into JPGs, many at once.

Processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Choose one or many HEIC photos, or drop them here.
  2. Press Convert to JPG.
  3. Download each photo, or all of them at once as a zip.

Good to know

The photos are converted in your browser, so they never leave your device. That also means nobody else ever has a copy of your family pictures.

The JPG does not contain the original's location or other camera details. If you need those, keep the HEIC too.

Questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. They're converted in your browser, on your device.

Will I lose quality?

Hardly at all at the default High setting. JPG is compressed slightly differently from HEIC, but you won't see it in normal use.

How many photos can I convert?

As many as your device's memory allows. Dozens at a time work fine; a phone is happier with smaller batches.

Is my location kept?

No. The JPGs are made from the picture only, so location and camera details are not copied.
