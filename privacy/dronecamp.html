Privacy Policy — DroneCamp

Last updated 9 November 2026 · applies to DroneCamp for iOS

DroneCamp keeps your study progress on your iPhone. Webspind runs no server for this app and cannot see your answers. This page explains that in detail, and it exists because Apple requires a privacy policy for every app.

What the app stores

Your answers, the legs and drills you have finished, your flagged questions, your mock test results, the test date you set and your reminder time. All of it stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising ID, no fingerprinting and no analytics tool, and Webspind runs no server for this app.

Permissions

DroneCamp asks for permission to send notifications only if you turn on the daily study reminder in Settings. The reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. During a mock test the countdown can appear on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity, which also runs on your iPhone. The app does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or health data.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. To confirm and restore your purchase the app uses the service RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, details of your purchases (product, price, time and App Store receipt) and technical details such as app version and country. RevenueCat does not get your answers, your name or your email address. Webspind does not receive your name, your email address or your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Links out of the app

The Terms and Privacy links open in your browser, and so does any source link you tap. Those websites have their own privacy policies. The Part 107 text and the charts in the app are stored on your iPhone and need no connection.

Crash reports

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send me aggregated crash reports with the device model, iOS version and a stack trace. They contain no name, no email address and none of your answers.

Children

DroneCamp is not directed at children.

Delete your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it has stored. You can also clear your progress in the app's Settings. If you want your purchase details deleted at RevenueCat, write to me. If you are in the EU, you can complain to your data protection authority; in Denmark that is Datatilsynet.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the release notes mention it.

Questions, or want something deleted? Write to webspind@gmail.com and I will answer within two working days.
