Add page numbers to a PDF

Put page numbers on every page, where you want them.

Processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Add a PDF.
  2. Choose the position, the first number and the format.
  3. Press Start and download the numbered PDF.

Questions

Can I skip the cover page?

Yes. Tick Skip the first page, and numbering starts on page two.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The page loads the tools into your browser, and your files are opened and saved on your own device. You can disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded and the tools keep working.

Is there a file-size limit?

Only your device's memory. Most computers handle PDFs of several hundred pages. On a phone, very large files can be slow.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no trial and no watermark.
